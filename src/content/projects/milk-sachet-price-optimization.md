MS Data Science capstone at the Asian Institute of Management, delivered by a four-person team for a Philippine FMCG client: a market-share forecasting ensemble trained on 2017–2024 regional Nielsen data that cut MAPE to 9.13% from a 22.83% historical-mean baseline across five regional markets. Price simulations recommend region-specific cuts of about ₱1.6 that lift market share by 0.44–3.00 percentage points, delivered through a Streamlit stakeholder dashboard with simulation, optimization and SHAP interpretation tabs.
